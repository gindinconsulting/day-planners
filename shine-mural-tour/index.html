72600 Dr. MLK Jr St N · the finale3 walls
The Skyline Building — SHINE's anchor site

Three pieces at one address: Osiris Rain (Charlotte, NC — Italian-born, classically trained) on the main building's bands, Reiver on the back walls, and Alyssa Marie's community-painted "Happy Mural Project" on the north perimeter.

Osiris Rain: "My training is rooted in classical painting… but I'm equally interested in what happens when that representation begins to break apart — when an image becomes atmosphere, pattern, memory, or symbol."

Reiver is Lewis Hobson of Durham, England — Collective Durham Spray Paints member and project manager of the Spennymoor Mural Festival, the UK crew that taught a whole generation of northern English muralists to paint big.

Where2600 Dr. MLK Jr St N, St. Pete 📍
ArtistsOsiris Rain · Reiver (Lewis Hobson, Durham UK) · Alyssa Marie

ℹ️ Know before you roll

festival logistics

📅 The festival

  • SHINE 12 runs Nov 6–15, 2026 — 13 artists, 12th year, ~170 murals since 2015. All murals stay up forever; the paint just isn't dry yet.
  • Artists work in daylight hours at their assigned walls through Nov 15 — organizers explicitly say to walk up and ask questions ("you might have to yell if they're high on the lift").
  • Closing reception: Sat Nov 14, 5–8pm at the Morean Arts Center (712 Central Ave) — free, meet every muralist, same night as the holiday show opening. Best chance to see all 13 artists in one place.
  • See all 170 past SHINE murals on the interactive map: shineonmurals.com/pixelstix · official site shineonmurals.com · IG @shineonstpete

🚴 Route notes

  • Full loop: 6.0 mi, 17 min of actual pedaling — this is a boulevard stroll, not a ride. Allow 2–2.5 hrs with stops, 3+ with coffee and chats.
  • No-bike option: the stops 1→2→3 downtown trio is a 1.7-mi flat walk; or park once at The Factory (stop 5) and walk stops 4–6 (1 mi).
  • Ride order matters: this order follows 1st Ave and the trail corridor — the Google Maps button keeps you off Tyrone Blvd until the optional wildcard.
  • Time of day: Sunday 10am–4pm is the sweet spot — artists painting, temps in the low 80s, zero rain. After 5:45pm the walls are still photogenic but the lifts come down.
